How it works

Capture. Understand. Prove.

SENDSerene isn't a chatbot and it isn't a filing cabinet. It's the part in between - the thing that turns a hard day into something a tribunal panel can actually read.

Voice logging

Five minutes, while it's fresh

Most parents don't have time to keep a diary. They do have five minutes in the car after pick-up. SENDSerene turns that into a dated, searchable entry.

  • A five-minute window with a gentle warning before time runs out.
  • An editable transcript before anything is saved.
  • Automatic tagging against the four statutory SEND categories, correctable by the parent.
  • A clear flag for anything safeguarding-related.

Jargon decoder

FAQ

The questions parents actually ask

Is this legal advice?

No. The plain-English decodes are summaries, not interpretations of your legal position - for advice, IPSEA, SENDIASS or a solicitor are the right place to go.

Which languages are supported?

English, Somali, Urdu, Punjabi, Bengali and Polish - toggle and framework by KoomBei, translations supplied and validated by Zifankrah Ltd.

Who built this?
